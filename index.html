01 — Who Am I

A strategist who designs, and a designer who measures.

I’m Akshara — a brand strategist and designer in Mumbai. I work at the seam where a brand’s thinking meets the thing a customer actually sees.

Most of my work starts with the same question: why isn’t this working? Sometimes the answer is the product. Sometimes it is the offer. Sometimes it is that nobody ever wrote down what the brand actually stands for. My job is to find out which one it is, and then build the answer.

That means the work spans brand analysis, identity, campaigns, copy, direction and video — not because I collect disciplines, but because a campaign only works when all of them are pulling in the same direction.

02 — Why You Need Me

Four reasons this stops being a side project

01

The brief is usually wrong

Most calls start with a symptom — the ads are not converting, the launch flopped, the brand feels dated. The first job is finding the real cause, which is usually upstream of what you asked for.

02

One person, whole chain

Strategy, design, copy and direction without a relay race. The hook in the ad, the layout that carries it and the deck that pitches it are all the same idea, not three freelancers agreeing by email.

03

It has to perform, not just look good

I read the funnel. Every creative decision gets a benchmark and a reason, and the work is judged against behaviour rather than taste.

04

You get something you can keep using

Files, templates, guidelines and a handover that means the work still looks right six months later when somebody else is running it.
