01 — Who Am I

Akshara SainiCreative & Brand Strategist, Mumbai

I work on the problems where the brief, the design and the performance are the same conversation. Eight years of making things and proving them, split between brand studios and the client side of the table.

StrategyDesignCopyDirectionVideo

The short version

A brand strategist and designer who got into the discipline sideways — through mass communication, then through campaigns, then through the numbers those campaigns produced.

I studied Multimedia and Mass Communication at Mithibai College, where I got comfortable making things and slightly suspicious of work that only looked good. That suspicion became the method. Every project since has started with the same two questions: what are we trying to change, and how will we know it worked.

My work has covered hotels, flights and activities at Travelxp; retention, CRM and influencer campaigns at Sacred Grove; multi-channel digital work at VerTech India; and the good, unglamorous hands-on stuff — packaging, menus, collateral, social systems — with The Food Rath and Teloz Spaces.

What I care about is the seam. The point where a good strategy becomes a good layout, where a good layout becomes a good ad, and where a good ad becomes revenue. Most teams lose the thread in one of those transitions. I have built my practice around not losing it.
